## Overview

Explored alternatives to steel for **sustainable concrete structures** in extreme
conditions intensified by climate change, focusing on fiber-reinforced polymer (FRP)
reinforcement under marine exposure.

## Contributions

- Involved in the **instrumentation and compression testing** of 20+ uniquely
  conditioned steel- and FRP-reinforced columns.
- Played a pivotal role in the **characterization and fabrication** of rebars,
  concrete cylinders, and other necessary components.
- Analyzed the effect of reinforcing material and architecture on **structural
  confinement** in the post-peak and failure phases.
- Examined cross-sections of failed specimens using **microscopy and imaging**
  to study microstructural features.
