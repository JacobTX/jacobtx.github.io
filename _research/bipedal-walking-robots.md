## Overview

Developing a robotic testbed with a **soft foot-actuation mechanism** for gait
initiation and bipedal walking studies — bridging mechanical design, electronics,
simulation, and control.

## Contributions

- Designed the **torso–leg system** and **foot-actuation mechanism** using DFMA
  and material/component-selection principles.
- Fabricated a customized **PCB in KiCAD** to efficiently route signals and
  streamline the electro-pneumatic subsystem.
- Developed a virtual **MATLAB environment** that simulates the actuation, swing,
  and impact mechanics of the biped robot.
- Implemented **HZD** (Hybrid Zero Dynamics) and **SLIP** control schemes for
  accurate torso stabilization and foot placement within **2% tolerance**.
