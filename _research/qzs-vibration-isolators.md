## Overview

Examined the efficacy of **Quasi-Zero Stiffness (QZS)** components for vibration
isolation in automotive applications, combining nonlinear dynamics modeling with
laboratory validation.

## Contributions

- Modeled and simulated the dynamics of a **QZS isolator stack–mass system** with
  manufacturing-induced heterogeneity.
- Investigated the excitation response of the isolator using **system
  linearization** and frequency- and physical-domain techniques.
- Used **data-based parameter tuning** and validated simulation results through
  vibration experiments, achieving **>85% match**.
- Analyzed the effect of damping parameters and operating regime on
  **transmissibility metrics** for improved isolator design.
