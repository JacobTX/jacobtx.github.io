## Overview

Investigated strategies to aid the **cost-effective rehabilitation** of people with
motor disabilities in the Indian setting, using wearable inertial sensing to track
recovery remotely.

## Contributions

- Laid the foundation for a **data-acquisition and analysis pipeline** to monitor
  the rehabilitation process of stroke patients.
- Performed **multi-DoF experiments** on fabricated hinges and human upper limbs
  for state estimation using **IMU data**.
- Deployed algorithms that use estimated **IMU quaternion data** to extract
  kinematic parameters such as joint angles.
