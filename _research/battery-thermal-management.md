**Role:** Research Project &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Duration:** Jan 2023 – Dec 2023

## Overview

Devised novel and efficient **thermal-management schemes** for batteries in electric
vehicles (EVs) to ensure longevity, formulating the problem as a constrained
optimization over coolant flow.

## Contributions

- Proposed alternate models based on **equilibrium temperature** to quantify the
  thermal degradation of Li-ion batteries.
- Formulated a **nonlinear discrete optimization** problem to optimize the coolant
  flow rate and reduce operating cost.
- Obtained results indicating up to **5% cost reduction**, exploiting the trade-off
  between degradation and pumping power.

> This work is closely related to my conference publication on
> [intelligent thermal management of Li-ion batteries](/publications/).
