I am **Jacob Thomas Sony**, a final-year Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) student in
**Mechanical Engineering** at the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay**,
where I also pursue minor degrees in *Artificial Intelligence & Data Science* and
*Systems & Control Engineering*.

My work sits at the intersection of **mechanics, control, and computation** — I
enjoy understanding how materials, structures, and systems behave, and designing
ways to predict and steer that behavior using analytical, computational, and
experimental tools.

## Research Interests

- **Mechanics and control of materials, structures, and systems** — using
  analytical, computational, and empirical methods.
- **Applications** — environmental and energy sustainability, biomedical
  engineering and healthcare, robotics, and automobiles.
