**Course:** Laser Manufacturing &nbsp;|&nbsp;
**Guide:** Prof. Ramesh Singh &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Duration:** Jan 2022 – May 2022

- Performed **transient thermal FEA** simulations of laser heating of an aluminium
  block to study features like melt depth.
- Modeled the **laser engraving** process using analytical principles of heat
  transfer and compared the results with simulation.
- Carried out **experiments** to study the effect of parameters such as power on
  performance aspects like penetration depth.
