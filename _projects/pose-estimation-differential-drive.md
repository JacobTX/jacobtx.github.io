**Course:** Embedded Control and Robotics &nbsp;|&nbsp;
**Guide:** Prof. Leena Vacchani &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Duration:** Jul 2023 – Dec 2023

- Employed the **Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)** for localization and pose
  estimation using motion and sensor models.
- Utilized a smooth, **Lyapunov-based nonlinear feedback control** for full-state
  stabilization and steering of the vehicle.
- Incorporated **adaptive gain tuning** into the steering control, enabling
  successful path navigation with **<2% deviation**.
