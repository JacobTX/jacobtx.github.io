**Course:** Intelligent Feedback and Control &nbsp;|&nbsp;
**Guide:** Prof. Leena Vacchani &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Duration:** Jan 2024 – May 2024

- Created a training environment in **MATLAB** with a suitable reward function and
  appropriate action and observation spaces.
- Designed a continuous **Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG)** agent with
  proper Actor and Critic network architectures.
- Trained the model-free agent to **swing up and stabilize an inverted pendulum**
  subject to torque and other constraints.
- Achieved desirable results from different initial conditions with **<5%
  overshoot** while remaining robust to sensor noise.
