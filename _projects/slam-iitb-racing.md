**Role:** SLAM Engineer, IIT Bombay Racing &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Duration:** 2020 – 2022

IIT Bombay Racing is a cross-functional team of 70+ students designing and
manufacturing race cars for *Formula Student* competitions. *IIT B Racing
Driverless* is the **first Indian team** to participate in the Formula Student AI
(UK) competition at Silverstone.

- Contributed to the development of the **driverless race car**, incorporating
  end-to-end simulation and testing.
- Implemented some of the earliest **in-house simulator-based testing platforms**,
  such as track generators and state visualizers.
- Created and tested initial versions of the **SLAM module** for accurate vehicle
  localization and track mapping.
- Designed a pipeline for **sensor fusion and state estimation** using odometry and
  LiDAR together with vehicle dynamics.
- Secured **3rd place** in the *FSEV 2021 Software and Intelligence Integration*
  competition, sponsored by Ather Energy.
