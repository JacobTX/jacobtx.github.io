## Abstract

This work presents an intelligent thermal-management strategy for lithium-ion
batteries based on **temperature-responsive coolant flow control**. By adapting the
coolant flow to the battery's thermal state, the approach targets reduced thermal
degradation and improved operating efficiency for electric-vehicle battery packs.

The study is part of a broader line of work on the
[optimal control of battery thermal-management systems](/research/battery-thermal-management/).
